Hardware · 2026

Portable Bluetooth Speaker

A custom PCB designed in Altium that integrates two MAX98357A class-D amplifiers with a LILYGO TTGO ESP32 and its built-in display. C++ firmware on the ESP32 streams Bluetooth audio out over I2S to dual 4Ω speakers, with playback status and metadata rendered on-screen. The whole thing runs off a portable power bank and lives in a 3D-printed enclosure.
