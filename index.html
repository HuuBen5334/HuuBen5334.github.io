Hardware · 2026

Portable Bluetooth Speaker

A battery-powered speaker built around a custom 2-layer PCB designed in Altium, integrating an ESP32 with dual MAX98357A class-D amplifiers. C++ firmware (Arduino framework) streams Bluetooth audio over I2S to dual 5W speakers, with hardware interrupts and debouncing for the tactile play/pause buttons. An LCD renders real-time system status and playback metadata, and everything is housed in a 3D-printed enclosure modeled in SolidWorks.
